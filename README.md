## Features

- **Bookmarks** — Bookmark the materials needed to level up characters and weapons, as well as artifacts and furnishings. View them by purpose or by material.
- **Database** — Browse characters, weapons, materials, artifacts, and furnishing sets.
- **Daily materials** — See which talent and weapon materials are available today, and get notified on the days your bookmarked materials are available.
- **Tools** — Resin recovery time calculator and HoYoLAB Daily Check-In.
- **HoYoLAB link** — Sync character and weapon levels, item counts in your bag, and resin from HoYoLAB (global version only).
